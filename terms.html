Other Policies

At Aavira, we ensure that your luxury ethnic wear reaches you safely and promptly.

  • Dispatch Time: All orders are strictly processed and dispatched within 24-48 business hours.
  • Delivery Timeline: Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 working days anywhere in India.
  • Shipping Charges: We offer FREE Premium Shipping on all prepaid orders. For Cash on Delivery (COD), a nominal convenience fee is applicable.
  • Tracking: Once dispatched, a tracking link will be sent to your registered email and WhatsApp number.

To maintain the exclusivity and pristine quality of our luxury handcrafted pieces, we operate on a highly time-sensitive return framework.

  • 4-Hour Express Return Window: Due to the premium nature of our products, any return or exchange request must be reported and initiated strictly within 4 hours of the delivery timestamp.
  • Strict Adherence: Requests raised after the 4-hour window has passed will be automatically declined, and the product will not be eligible for a return under any circumstances.
  • Condition: The product must remain completely unused, unwashed, and secured in its original luxury packaging with all tamper-proof tags completely intact.
  • Non-Returnable Items: Custom-tailored products, accessories, and items bought during 'Clearance Sales' are strictly non-returnable.
  • Refund Process: Once approved and quality-checked at our warehouse, refunds are processed within 5-7 working days directly to your original payment source.
